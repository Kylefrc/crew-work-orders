# Heat Pump Water Heater
key: hpwh
applies: hvac
auto: hpwh|heat pump water heater
qa: true
order: 15

- Install space gives the unit 750+ cubic feet of surrounding air (or is ducted per manufacturer)
- Emergency drain pan under the tank with a 3/4"+ drain line run to a drain
- Temperature set at 125°F or lower
- Pressure relief valve installed per manufacturer
- Relief discharge tube ends within 6" of the floor
- Thermal expansion tank installed when the water supply is a closed system
- Accessible hot water pipes insulated to R-3 minimum
- Condensate drain run per local code
- Unit set to EFFICIENCY / HEAT PUMP mode — hybrid and electric-resistance modes stay off
- Dedicated electrical circuit verified at the panel
