- Mold, moisture, and pest damage corrected BEFORE any insulation goes in
- Attic ventilation verified at {vent_ratio} net free area with bug screens on vents — confirm local code
- Attic access lid insulated R-38+ and weatherstripped airtight
- Ductwork in the attic sealed (mastic/UL-181), insulated R-8, and supported
- Knee walls and skylight wells insulated R-21+ with an attic-side air barrier
- Insulation dams around chimneys and flues in heat-safe material
- Non-airtight recessed cans boxed over, seams sealed
- Blown to the specified R-value at Grade 1 — even depth, no low spots
- Baffles at every eave bay, depth markers visible (minimum 3)
