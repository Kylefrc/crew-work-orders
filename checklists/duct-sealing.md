- Ducts outside conditioned space sealed
- Ducts outside conditioned space insulated to R-8
- Leakage tested BEFORE and AFTER — record CFM25 both times
- Duct supports every 4 feet or less, sag under 1/2" per foot
- Duct boots in unconditioned spaces sealed to the finished surface
