- ENERGY STAR certified (Q89)

- Duct terminates OUTDOORS — clear of attic, crawl, and floor cavities (Q91)
- First 3 feet of duct off the fan run straight (Q92)
- Outlet 10+ ft from any air inlet (Q93)
- All duct seams sealed mastic/UL-181 (Q94)
- Wall cap includes damper (Q95)
