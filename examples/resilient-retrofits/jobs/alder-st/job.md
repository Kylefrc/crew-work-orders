## Scope of Work

| Trade | Task | Details | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| hvac | Remove gas furnace | Cap gas at the appliance, remove furnace + attic duct runs, haul away | Hall closet + attic |
| hvac | Install ductless heat pump | 24k cold-climate outdoor unit on east wall pad, 12k wall head (living), 9k wall head (bedroom), linesets through attic | East wall, living room, bedroom |
| hvac | Range hood duct | Duct new 300 CFM hood through upper cabinet, out the wall cap with damper | Kitchen |
| hvac | Bath fan | ENERGY STAR 110 CFM fan with humidistat, duct out the roof, first 3 ft straight | Hall bath |
| wx | Attic air seal | Full ceiling plane: top plates, can lights, penetrations, flue dam; hold the hood + bath-fan duct spots Day 1 and close them after HVAC runs ducts Day 2 | Attic |
| wx | Attic prep + insulation | Baffles at every eave bay, hatch dam + weatherstrip, 3 depth markers, then blow R-49 cellulose over 940 sf | Attic |
| elec | New 240V circuit for heat pump | 25A 2-pole breaker, 10/2 from panel to east-wall disconnect, whip to ODU | Panel (garage) to east wall |
